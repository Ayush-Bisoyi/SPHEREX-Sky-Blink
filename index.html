Spectrum at the centre · MJy/sr above local background

One point per displayed frame, so adding exposures or stacking changes it (fewer, less noisy points). Colours are detectors D1–D6. It describes whatever sits at the ring, so it is only meaningful for a stationary source; field of view does not change it.

Larger fields catch faster movers but download more (up to 30′). Each exposure samples a different wavelength, so steady stars can still change brightness. "Subtract static sky" removes the per-detector median: movers show as a bright/dark pair. Gaia and WISE positions are from their own epochs (2016, ~2010), so movers can sit away from their markers.
